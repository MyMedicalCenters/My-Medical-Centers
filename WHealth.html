Focused Practice

Dr. Keiko Chan

Focused Practice in Gynaecology

Dr. Keiko Chan completed medical school in Calgary and residency in Family Medicine in Ottawa, training extensively in women's health at the Shirley E Greenberg Center. She is passionate about making procedures like IUD insertions, endometrial biopsies, and Pap tests comfortable, offering cervical nerve blocks, inhaled Penthrox, and anxiolytic medication options. She frequently lectures on perimenopause/menopause and holds Menopause Society certification.

Focused Practice

Dr. Sawini Fernando

Focused Practice in Gynaecology

Dr. Sawini Fernando graduated from Dalhousie University's medical school and completed Family Medicine residency and Enhanced Skills Training in Women's Health at Queen's University. She is dedicated to empathetic, trauma-informed gynecologic care for cisgender, transgender, and non-binary individuals, offering tailored pain management including paracervical blocks during clinical procedures.

Conditions Evaluated & Procedures Offered

We provide specialized consultations, diagnostics, and procedures in a welcoming outpatient setting:

📋
Pap Smear & HPV Testing Screening, follow-ups, and cervical health evaluations.
💊
Contraceptive Consultations IUD insertions & removals (Mirena, Kyleena, Copper), Nexplanon, oral contraceptives.
🛡️
Advanced Pain Management Cervical nerve blocks, inhaled Penthrox, and premedication for maximum comfort.
🩸
Menstrual Disorders & PCOS Irregular, painful, or heavy bleeding, endometriosis, and adenomyosis workups.
🔬
Endometrial Biopsy Investigation of abnormal post-menopausal or heavy uterine bleeding.
🧠
PMDD & Severe Mood Disorders Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder assessments and targeted medical treatment.
🌿
Perimenopause & Menopause Evidence-based hormone therapy (HRT) and non-hormonal symptom management.
🩺
Medical Abortion (<10 Weeks) Confidential, compassionate outpatient early pregnancy termination support.

How to Submit a Referral

We accept physician and nurse practitioner referrals exclusively through OceanMD eReferral (completely free for the sender).

  1. Log in or create a free account on OceanMD.
  2. Click the Ocean referral link or open the eReferral directory.
  3. Make sure your selected location is set near Ottawa.
  4. In the "Find any health service" search bar, type: My Medical Centers - Women's Health Keiko Chan.
  5. Open the provider entry and click Send eReferral at the bottom of their page.
