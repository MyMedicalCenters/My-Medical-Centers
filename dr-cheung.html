👨‍⚕️ About Dr. Dennis Cheung

Dr. Dennis Cheung is a Fellow with the College of Family Physicians of Canada (FCFP) and a Fellow of the American Academy of Family Physicians (FAAFP). He has advanced clinical training and special interests in integumentary medicine, skin surgery, and integumentary procedures, along with a wide spectrum of specialized gynecological, male sexual health, and therapeutic injection procedures.

📋 Summary of Services & Procedures Offered

Comprehensive diagnostic assessments, skin surgeries, and minimally invasive clinical procedures performed in a welcoming outpatient setting.
