We Accept Referrals Through OceanMD (Free for Senders)

Physicians and nurse practitioners can easily submit electronic referrals through OceanMD.

How to send a referral (quick steps):

  1. Sign in (or sign up for free) to your Clinic Account on the top right of OceanMD.
  2. Click the OceanMD link below to open the referral portal.
  3. Make sure your selected location is set near Ottawa.
  4. In the "Find any health service" search bar, type: Dr. Olayemi Sadiku.
  5. Open the provider entry and click the Send eReferral button at the bottom of the page to complete your referral.

Clinical Scope & Areas of Focus

Dr. Sadiku provides comprehensive diagnostic consultations, medication optimization, and evidence-based management for:

Mood Disorders

Major Depressive Disorder, treatment-resistant depression, bipolar disorder (type I and II), and dysthymia.

Anxiety & Trauma

Generalized anxiety disorder, panic disorder, social anxiety, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), and PTSD.

Psychotic Spectrum Disorders

Schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder, and delusional disorders in stable outpatient settings.

Complex Psychiatric Consults

Diagnostic clarification, psychopharmacology second opinions, and multi-condition management.
