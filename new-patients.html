How New Patient Registration Works

Enrolling with a family physician at My Medical Centers is quick, digital, and streamlined:

1

Choose Your Location

Select the clinic closest to you: Carling (Ottawa Central), Richmond (Ottawa West), or Orléans (Ottawa East).

2

Complete Digital Intake

Click the registration button to open our secure Ocean booking portal. Provide your valid provincial health card (OHIP, RAMQ, etc.) or private coverage details.

3

Meet Your Physician

Schedule your first intake visit to complete medical history documentation and establish longitudinal healthcare coverage with your dedicated doctor.
